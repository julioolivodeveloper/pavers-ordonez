5 Paver Walkway Designs That Instantly Boost Curb Appeal

July 8, 2025 4 min read Pavers Ordonez Team

Your home's walkway is more than a path from the street to your front door — it's a design statement. A well-designed paver walkway can increase your home's perceived value, make a lasting impression on guests, and transform your entire front yard. Here are the five walkway designs our Palm Beach County customers love most.

1. Classic Herringbone Brick Walkway

The herringbone pattern — alternating bricks laid at 45 or 90-degree angles — is one of the most popular and enduring walkway designs in South Florida. It creates a visually dynamic path that draws the eye forward and adds architectural character to any home style, from Mediterranean to contemporary.

Herringbone walkways work especially well with red or tan brick pavers. Adding a contrasting double-soldier border in a different color or material elevates the design significantly. This pattern also happens to be very stable structurally — the interlocking geometry distributes weight evenly and resists shifting over time.

2. Running Bond with Wide Joints

The running bond pattern — offset bricks in straight rows — looks clean, modern, and timeless. For a contemporary West Palm Beach aesthetic, we recommend using large-format concrete pavers (18"x36" or bigger) with 3/8" joints filled with polymeric sand. The wider spacing creates a sophisticated grid effect that photographs beautifully and pairs perfectly with modern landscaping.

3. Curved Natural Stone Stepping Path

If your yard has a more organic, garden-style feel, a curved path with large natural stone or travertine steppers set into grass or gravel is a stunning choice. This design feels relaxed and luxurious at the same time — like something out of an upscale Florida resort.

The key to making this work is proper spacing (about 18–24 inches between centers, matching a natural walking stride) and setting each stone level and stable. Loose or uneven steppers become a trip hazard quickly, so professional installation matters here.

4. Bordered Fan or Circular Pattern

For homes with a wide front entryway, a fan or circular paver pattern at the front door creates a dramatic focal point. This is a more complex installation — it requires custom cutting — but the result is extraordinary. We've completed several of these in Palm Beach Gardens and Boca Raton and they're always showstoppers.

Design Tip: Use a lighter colored paver for the field pattern and a darker or contrasting color for the border. This separation makes both elements pop and gives the whole walkway a finished, high-end look.

5. Two-Tone Parallel Bands

One of the trendiest walkway styles in 2025 is the two-tone banded design: alternating rows of two different paver colors running parallel to the path. This creates a modern, resort-style look that's especially popular in newer construction homes across the western Palm Beach County communities.

Common combinations include charcoal + buff, ivory + terra cotta, or gray + sand. The pattern is relatively simple to install but delivers maximum visual impact for the investment.

What Makes the Biggest Difference

Across all five designs, the details that separate a good walkway from a great one are: consistent joint width, proper base depth (4–6 inches of compacted base rock), correct drainage slope (1/8" to 1/4" per foot away from the house), and quality polymeric sand to lock everything in place.
