Why Pavers

Benefits of Paver Patios

Stays Cooler in the Sun

Unlike dark concrete, lighter paver options like travertine reflect heat — making your outdoor space comfortable even on the hottest Florida days.

Slip-Resistant Surface

Textured paver surfaces provide excellent grip when wet — essential for pool decks and areas exposed to Florida's frequent rain showers.

No Cracking

Interlocking pavers flex and move with the ground, eliminating the cracking and heaving that plagues poured concrete patios in Florida's shifting soil.

Endless Design Options

Combine colors, sizes, and patterns to create a truly custom outdoor space. Add borders, inlays, and focal points that make your patio unique.

Boosts Property Value

A professionally designed paver patio is one of the highest-ROI outdoor improvements, adding real value and selling appeal to your home.

Easy to Maintain

Sealed paver patios resist stains, mildew, and discoloration. Cleaning is as simple as hosing down or pressure washing when needed.

How We Do It

Our Patio Installation Process

1

Design Consultation

We visit your home, walk the backyard with you, discuss your vision, and take measurements. We'll suggest layouts, patterns, and materials that work with your space and budget.

2

Site Preparation & Grading

We excavate to the correct depth, ensure proper drainage slopes (critical in Florida), and grade the area so water flows away from your home and pool area.

3

Base & Sand Installation

A compacted crushed stone base is installed followed by a screeded sand layer — the foundation that keeps your patio flat, stable, and weed-resistant for years.

4

Paver Laying & Edge Restraints

Pavers are placed in your chosen pattern, borders are installed, and all edges are secured with professional-grade edge restraints to prevent spreading.

5

Final Compaction, Jointing & Sealing

We compact, fill joints with polymeric sand to inhibit weeds and insects, then apply sealer for color enhancement and long-term protection from Florida's elements.

Common Questions

Patio Pavers FAQ

What type of paver is best for a pool deck in Florida?

Travertine is a top choice for Florida pool decks — it stays naturally cool, is slip-resistant, and won't fade or crack like concrete. We also install concrete and tumbled pavers for pool decks depending on your style and budget.

How long does patio installation take?

Most patio projects take 2–5 days depending on size and complexity. A large pool deck with multiple areas may take up to a week. We'll give you a precise timeline during your estimate.

Do I need a permit for a paver patio in Palm Beach County?

Permit requirements vary by city and project size. We're familiar with local regulations and can advise you on what's needed for your specific project during the consultation.

Will my patio have drainage issues during heavy Florida rains?

Not when installed correctly. We grade all patio surfaces to slope away from your home and pool, and use joint patterns that allow for proper water flow. Permeable paver options are also available.
