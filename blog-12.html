Travertine vs. Concrete Pavers: Which Is Right for Your West Palm Beach Home?

July 28, 2025 6 min read Pavers Ordonez Team

One of the most common questions we get from Palm Beach County homeowners is: "Should I go with travertine or concrete pavers?" Both are excellent options, but they're right for different situations, budgets, and aesthetics. Here's our honest comparison after installing thousands of square feet of both.

Travertine Pavers

Travertine is a natural limestone formed by mineral springs. It has a distinctive vein-like texture, a naturally cool surface even in direct sun, and a timeless elegance that's hard to replicate artificially. It's the paver of choice for luxury homes, pool decks, and Mediterranean-style properties across South Florida.

Pros: Stays cool barefoot in summer heat (a major advantage for pool decks), naturally slip-resistant, unique appearance, excellent resale value, salt-air resistant.

Cons: Higher cost ($18–$32 per sq ft installed), requires sealing to prevent staining, limited color options (ivory, walnut, noce, gold), heavier — requires a stronger base.

Concrete Pavers

Concrete pavers are manufactured units made from a high-strength concrete mix with pigment added for color. They're extremely versatile, available in hundreds of shapes and colors, and significantly more affordable than natural stone. They're the most popular choice for driveways and patios across West Palm Beach for good reason.

Pros: Cost-effective ($10–$22 per sq ft installed), enormous variety of colors and shapes, consistent sizing makes installation precise, can be made to look like brick, cobblestone, or large-format tile.

Cons: Can fade faster than natural stone without sealing, may absorb heat more than travertine, manufactured appearance won't fool a trained eye.

Head-to-Head: Which Wins?

Pool Decks: Travertine wins. Its natural cool surface and slip resistance are hard to beat for bare feet.
Driveways: Concrete pavers win. They handle heavy vehicle loads better and cost significantly less.
Patios: Depends on your budget and style — both work beautifully.
Walkways: Concrete pavers win on cost; travertine wins on luxury appearance.

Our Recommendation

If budget is the primary concern, go concrete — you'll get a beautiful result at a lower price point. If you're doing a pool deck or a high-visibility area where you want maximum curb appeal and you're willing to invest, travertine is worth every dollar.
