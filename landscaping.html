Common Questions

Landscaping FAQ

What type of grass do you recommend for West Palm Beach?

St. Augustine (especially Floratam) is the most popular choice in Palm Beach County — it handles heat, humidity, and partial shade well. For full-sun areas with heavier traffic, Zoysia is an excellent alternative. We'll recommend the best option based on your yard's specific conditions.

Can you fix my yard's drainage problem?

Yes. Standing water after rain is a common problem in South Florida. We assess the cause — whether it's poor grading, clay soil, or inadequate drainage — and install the right solution: French drains, catch basins, swales, or regrading. We fix the root cause, not just the symptoms.

Do you use native Florida plants?

Yes, and we recommend them whenever possible. Native Florida plants are adapted to local rainfall patterns, require less irrigation and fertilizer, and support local wildlife. They're also more resistant to pests and disease than non-native species. We'll suggest native options that fit your aesthetic goals.

Can you do landscaping and pavers at the same time?

Absolutely — and we encourage it. Combining both projects saves time, money, and the headache of coordinating multiple contractors. We design pavers and landscaping to complement each other perfectly, and our crew handles everything in one organized process.

How much does landscaping cost in West Palm Beach?

Landscaping costs vary widely depending on yard size, services needed, and materials chosen. We provide free, detailed written estimates for every project — contact us and we'll visit your property to give you an accurate quote at no charge.
