How to Choose the Right Pavers for Your Driveway in West Palm Beach

July 15, 2025 5 min read Pavers Ordonez Team West Palm Beach, FL

Choosing the right paver material for your driveway is one of the most important decisions you'll make for your home's exterior. In West Palm Beach, the combination of intense heat, heavy summer rain, and high humidity puts pavers through serious stress — and not every material handles it equally well.

After completing hundreds of driveway installations across Palm Beach County, we've seen what works and what doesn't. Here's an honest breakdown of your best options.

Concrete Pavers: The Most Popular Choice in West Palm Beach

Concrete interlocking pavers are by far the most common choice for driveways in our area — and for good reason. They're durable, affordable, and available in dozens of colors and shapes. Unlike poured concrete, interlocking pavers flex with the ground, meaning they won't crack as easily when the soil shifts during Florida's wet and dry seasons.

Cost typically runs between $12–$20 per square foot installed, making it the most budget-friendly option among premium materials. Maintenance is minimal: annual sealing and occasional sand replacement in the joints is usually all that's needed.

Pro Tip: Darker concrete pavers absorb more heat. In West Palm Beach's climate, consider lighter shades or textured surfaces that stay cooler underfoot.

Brick Pavers: Classic Look with Proven Durability

Genuine clay brick pavers have been used for driveways for over a century — and there's a reason they've stood the test of time. They're extremely dense, resistant to fading, and actually get better-looking as they age. In Florida's UV-intense environment, brick won't lose its color the way some concrete pavers can.

The trade-off is cost: brick typically runs $15–$25 per square foot installed. They're also heavier, requiring a more robust base to prevent settling. But for homeowners who want a timeless, high-end look, brick delivers it without question.

Travertine: Premium Beauty, but Know the Limitations

Travertine is a natural stone that looks absolutely stunning — and in West Palm Beach, it's particularly popular for pool decks and patios. For driveways, however, we recommend it with caution. Travertine is porous, which means it can absorb oil and stain more easily than concrete or brick. It also requires more frequent sealing (every 1–2 years) to stay looking its best.

That said, if you seal it regularly and avoid heavy vehicle traffic in certain conditions, travertine can be a beautiful driveway material. Cost ranges from $20–$35 per square foot installed.

Porcelain Pavers: The New Premium Option

Porcelain pavers have surged in popularity over the last few years, and for good reason. They're virtually non-porous, stain-resistant, and fade-proof — ideal for Florida's climate. Porcelain handles high heat well, can be textured for slip resistance, and comes in large format sizes that create a very modern, clean aesthetic.

The downside is cost: expect $25–$45 per square foot installed. Installation also requires more precision and expertise. But if you want a low-maintenance, long-lasting driveway with a contemporary look, porcelain is hard to beat.

Our Recommendation for West Palm Beach Homeowners

For most homeowners in our area, concrete interlocking pavers offer the best balance of cost, durability, and aesthetics. If you have a larger budget and want something truly special, brick or porcelain are excellent upgrades. Reserve travertine for pool decks and patios where it truly shines.

The most important factor is installation quality. Even the best paver material will fail if the base preparation isn't done correctly. Always hire a licensed contractor who uses proper compaction, drainage slope, and polymeric sand.
