How We Do It

Our Installation Process

A proven step-by-step process that guarantees a driveway built to last.

1

Site Evaluation & Design

We visit your property, measure the area, assess the base condition, and discuss your style preferences. We'll help you choose the right paver type, color, and pattern for your home.

2

Excavation & Base Preparation

We excavate the area to the correct depth and install a compacted gravel base — the most critical step for a long-lasting driveway that won't sink or shift over time.

3

Sand Bedding Layer

A layer of leveling sand is applied over the gravel base and carefully screeded to create a perfectly flat, even surface for the pavers.

4

Paver Installation & Cutting

Pavers are laid in your chosen pattern, cut precisely at edges with a masonry saw, and checked for level throughout the installation.

5

Compaction, Jointing & Sealing

We compact the pavers into the sand, sweep polymeric sand into the joints to lock everything in place, and apply a professional-grade sealer for protection and shine.

Common Questions

Driveway Pavers FAQ

How long does a paver driveway installation take?

Most residential driveway installations take 2–4 days depending on size. We'll give you an accurate timeline during your free estimate visit.

How long do paver driveways last?

A properly installed and sealed paver driveway can last 25–50 years or more. Individual pavers can be replaced if needed, extending the life of the driveway indefinitely.

Do paver driveways require maintenance?

Minimal maintenance is needed. We recommend re-sealing every 2–3 years to protect color and prevent staining. Occasional weeding of joint sand may be needed, which polymeric sand greatly reduces.

Can pavers be installed over existing concrete?

In some cases, yes — if the existing concrete is structurally sound and level. We'll assess your current surface and recommend the best approach during your estimate.
