The Best Paver Patterns for Driveways and Patios in West Palm Beach

April 10, 2025 4 min read Pavers Ordonez Team

The pattern you choose for your paver installation isn't just about aesthetics — it also affects the structural performance of the surface. Some patterns distribute load better than others, making them smarter choices for driveways where vehicles will repeatedly park and drive. Here's a breakdown of the most popular patterns and which applications each one suits best.

Herringbone: The Gold Standard for Driveways

Herringbone is widely considered the strongest paver pattern for vehicle traffic. The interlocking 45° or 90° angles create a load-distributing network that resists shifting under repeated heavy weight. When a vehicle drives over a herringbone surface, the compressive forces transfer laterally through the angled joints rather than pushing straight down through any single joint line.

This makes herringbone the pattern of choice for driveways, commercial parking areas, and any surface that sees heavy or frequent vehicle traffic. It looks classic, works with almost every paver type and color, and remains the most popular pattern in Palm Beach County driveways by a wide margin.

Best for: Driveways, commercial parking, high-traffic areas
Cost impact: Moderate (more cuts than running bond)

Running Bond: Clean and Contemporary

Running bond — alternating rows offset by half a paver — is the most recognizable pattern and the easiest to install, which makes it one of the more affordable options. It has a clean, linear look that works particularly well with large-format modern pavers (12"x24" or larger) and contemporary home architecture.

Running bond is ideal for patios, pool decks, and walkways. For driveways, it works fine with proper base preparation, but herringbone provides better structural performance under repeated vehicle loads.

Best for: Patios, pool decks, walkways, modern-style driveways
Cost impact: Lowest (minimal cuts, efficient installation)

Popular Combo: Many homeowners use running bond for the field (main area) and add a contrasting single or double soldier-course border in a different color. This simple addition dramatically elevates the finished look.

Basket Weave: Traditional Charm

Basket weave pairs bricks in alternating perpendicular groups of two, creating a woven appearance that's especially popular with traditional, Colonial, and Mediterranean-style homes across Palm Beach County. It requires a uniform rectangular paver where the length equals exactly twice the width (typically 4"x8" or 3.75"x7.5").

Basket weave looks beautiful for front walkways and garden paths, but is less common for driveways because the pattern doesn't interlock as effectively as herringbone under vehicle loads.

Best for: Walkways, garden paths, patios with traditional home styles
Cost impact: Moderate

Fan / Cobblestone: Maximum Visual Impact

Fan patterns (also called Venetian fan or Catherine wheel) use specially shaped pavers to create circular, radial designs. They're stunning, rare, and immediately make a property stand out. For a front entry, circular driveway apron, or courtyard centerpiece, a fan pattern is as close to paving as art as you'll get.

The trade-off: fan patterns require the most skill to install, generate significant cutting waste, and cost 25–40% more in labor than standard patterns. Not every contractor can execute them properly.

Best for: Courtyard focal points, circular driveways, entry accents
Cost impact: Highest

Random / Ashlar: Natural and Organic

The random (or ashlar) pattern uses multiple paver sizes laid in an intentionally irregular arrangement that mimics natural stone. It's the most natural-looking option and pairs beautifully with Florida's tropical landscaping. The key is that "random" doesn't mean haphazard — achieving a truly balanced random layout that doesn't repeat visually requires skill and planning.

This pattern works well for patios, pool decks, and garden areas where a more relaxed, organic feel is desired. It's less common for driveways but works for lighter-traffic areas.

Best for: Patios, pool decks, walkways with natural landscaping
Cost impact: Moderate to high

Our Recommendation

For most West Palm Beach homeowners: herringbone for driveways, running bond or random for patios and pool decks. Add a contrasting border to either and the result is a professionally finished installation that looks custom without the custom price tag.
