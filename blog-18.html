Pool Deck Pavers: Best Materials for West Palm Beach Pools

May 15, 2025 5 min read Pavers Ordonez Team

Your pool deck is one of the most used — and most scrutinized — surfaces in your South Florida home. It needs to look beautiful, stay cool underfoot in the summer heat, be slip-resistant when wet, and stand up to constant exposure to pool chemicals, UV radiation, and foot traffic. Here's what we recommend for West Palm Beach pools.

The Non-Negotiables for Pool Deck Pavers

Before choosing a material, every pool deck paver should meet these requirements: slip resistance when wet (ANSI/DCOF rating of 0.42 or higher), heat resistance (doesn't get painfully hot in direct sun), pool chemical resistance (doesn't stain or degrade from chlorine or salt water), and durability (rated for freeze-thaw, though less critical in South Florida).

1. Travertine — The Gold Standard

Travertine is the most popular pool deck material in Palm Beach County for very good reason. Its naturally porous surface stays remarkably cool even in direct sun — something no manufactured paver can fully replicate. The tumbled finish provides natural slip resistance, and its classic appearance complements virtually every architectural style.

Best for: Luxury pools, coastal properties, Mediterranean and contemporary homes.
Cost: $18–$30 per sq ft installed.
Colors available: Ivory, Walnut, Noce, Gold, Silver.

2. Porcelain Pavers — The Low-Maintenance Option

Large-format porcelain pavers (24"x24" or 24"x48") have become extremely popular in the last few years. They're non-porous (no staining, no sealing required), fade-resistant, and available in stunning marble-look, concrete-look, and wood-look finishes. They're also lighter than travertine, making them easier to install on elevated pool decks.

Best for: Modern and contemporary homes, low-maintenance-focused clients.
Cost: $20–$35 per sq ft installed.
Note: Choose textured (not polished) porcelain for pool areas.

3. Concrete Pavers — The Value Option

Quality concrete pavers from manufacturers like Belgard or Unilock can make excellent pool decks at a lower price point. Look for a brushed or exposed aggregate finish for slip resistance. Standard smooth concrete pavers get too hot in direct Florida sun and can be slippery when wet.

Best for: Budget-conscious homeowners who still want a polished look.
Cost: $12–$20 per sq ft installed.

Important: Whatever material you choose, make sure the coping (the edge pieces around the pool itself) matches or complements your deck pavers. The coping is what guests see most, and a mismatch is noticeable immediately.

Drainage Considerations for Pool Decks

Pool decks require a minimum 1% slope away from the pool to prevent water from draining back in. For larger decks, we often incorporate channel drains or catch basins at strategic low points. Proper drainage also prevents the deck from becoming slippery and reduces algae growth.
